# Everyday_Hero
For Matthew Fallon and Colin Zopf's Portfolios.
This project was a Collaboration between 2 students from the Rochester Institute of Technology.

The goal of the project was to test our individual skills in a semi - professional environment, with professional guidance from experienced teachers at RIT,
We aimed to create a demo for an idea we had for a game, Everyday Hero.

Everyday Hero is a fantasy themed Hero Management Simulator, sharing similarities with the game Dispatch.
The core gameplay centers on recieving quests, appropreately assigning them to waiting heros, sending support to heroes when they need it, and recieving commission
based on your work for the day, before going home to sleep for the day.

We have 3 future goals with this project:
1) Fully flush out the story we wish to tell: While our heroes have some personality, they are lacking a cohesive connecting narrative between the player, themselves, and their relation to their work.
2) Updating our art style: as a 2 man team, with neither of us specializing in drawing, most art we utilize were made with pixilart software.
While we wish to keep that style, upscaling the art & adding more animations would immensely improve the feel of the game.
3) Proper musical score: While we originally had a teammate who specialized in sound design, they were pulled away into another opportunity, and relinquished credit on this project in June of 2025. as such,
we utilized AI systems to create tunes that convey the message we wish for the music to convey, but we wish to recruit a composer to create humane music for this project.

Development on this project began in May of 2025.
You can find our official up-to-date builds of Everyday Hero at our itch.io page here: https://everydayheros.itch.io/everyday-hero
